This is the first of a two-part course in mathematical statistics. After reviewing probability, we study sampling distributions, the delta method, parametric estimation, maximum likelihood and Bayesian methods, confidence intervals, and hypothesis testing.

## References

- Morris H. DeGroot and Mark J. Schervish, *Probability and Statistics*.
- Robert V. Hogg, Joseph W. McKean, and Allen T. Craig, *Introduction to Mathematical Statistics* (HMC).
- John A. Rice, *Mathematical Statistics and Data Analysis*.
- Larry Wasserman, *All of Statistics*.

I graciously acknowledge Zhou Fan, Cheng Mao, and Yihong Wu for sharing their teaching materials, which served as the inspiration for this course.
